📖 How to Play & Scoring / قواعد اللعبة والنقاط

  • Base & Speed Scoring / النقاط وسرعة الإجابة: Correct answers earn +1 pt. Answering quickly (or locking in first in Live Zoom Mode) awards a ⚡ +1 Speed Bonus!
  • Zoom Breakout Room Mode / وضع غرف الزوم: Each Breakout Room Captain opens their team's Room Link, shares their screen with teammates to read the full bilingual question, and locks in their choice independently.
  • Streak Multiplier / مكافأة التتابع: Reaching 3+ consecutive correct answers (🔥) awards an extra +1 Streak Bonus.
  • Final Question Finale / الجولة الختامية: The last question of every match automatically awards 2x Double Points!
