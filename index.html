📖 How to Play & Scoring / قواعد اللعبة والنقاط

  • Base & Speed Scoring / النقاط وسرعة الإجابة: Correct answers earn +1 pt. Answering quickly (or locking in first in Live Modes) awards a ⚡ +1 Speed Bonus!
  • 3 Game Modes / ثلاثة أنماط للعب: Play turn-based on one screen, compete as 2 Teams in Zoom Breakout Rooms, or let Individual Students join with their own names via QR Code / Room Code!
  • Al-Husary Audio Listening Rounds / جولات الاستماع للحصري: Listen to Shaykh Al-Husary's authentic recitation and identify the Tajweed rule applied in the verse.
  • Streak & Finale / التتابع والجولة الختامية: 3+ consecutive correct answers (🔥) award +1 Streak Bonus, and the last question awards 2x Double Points!
